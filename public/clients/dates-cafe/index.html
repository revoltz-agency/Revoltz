Good to know

Before you send your request.

A few quick answers about how slot requests work.

Is my slot booked immediately?

No. Sending a request does not reserve a slot automatically. Date's Cafe needs to review it and confirm with you.

What happens when I tap “Prepare booking request”?

Your device will open its default email app with the details filled in. Review the message and tap Send to submit it.

What if my email app doesn't open?

Make sure an email app is set up on your device. If it still doesn't work, contact the cafe directly using the contact details shared by the cafe.

Can I change my preferred time?

Yes. Send another request with your updated preference, or reply to the cafe when they contact you.
